## What Makes Good Website Hosting for Small Business?

**Good small business hosting is fast, reliable, secure and affordable, with business email, backups, free SSL and helpful support.**

Look for:

- **Fast loading speeds**, ideally with SSD or NVMe storage
- **99.9% uptime** or better
- **Free SSL** to keep your site secure
- **Business email** with your own domain
- **Automatic backups**
- **24/7 support**
- **Fair renewal prices**
- **Easy upgrades** as your business grows

## Which Type of Hosting Is Best for a Small Business?

**Most small businesses should start with shared or managed WordPress hosting, then move to cloud or VPS hosting as traffic grows.**

- **Shared hosting:** Cheapest, good for new and small sites
- **Managed WordPress hosting:** Less maintenance for WordPress sites
- **Cloud hosting:** Best for online stores and traffic spikes
- **VPS hosting:** More control for growing or custom sites

## FAQs

### What is the best small business web hosting?
Hostinger is the best overall value for most small businesses, while SiteGround is best for support.

### What is the cheapest web hosting for small business?
Hostinger and Namecheap both start at about $2 a month on long plans.

### Do small businesses need managed hosting?
Not always. Shared hosting works for small sites, but managed WordPress hosting saves time on updates and backups.

### Does small business hosting include email?
Many plans do, including Hostinger and Namecheap shared hosting. EasyWP and some GoDaddy plans don't.

### Is shared hosting good for a small business website?
Yes. Shared hosting is fine for most new small business websites.

### When should a small business upgrade to cloud hosting?
When your site slows down, gets traffic spikes or runs an online store.

### How much does website hosting for small business cost?
Usually $2 to $10 a month at first, but renewal prices are often much higher.

### What should I look for in small business hosting?
Speed, uptime, free SSL, business email, backups, support and fair renewal prices.
